After an impressive season by both the Beer Gut Mutts and the Purple Cobras (both finished the season with a 10-4 record and winning their division), these two teams met in the MFFL Championship! Unfortunately for the Mutts, they aren't able to get back to back championships as the Purple Cobras come out striking and put up the 3rd best point total across the league for the entire season! This capped off a great season for the Cobras and awarded them their first ever MFFL Championship!

At the other end of the bracket, the Scranton Stranglers and the Nightmares face off each of them hoping to avoid taking home the Toilet Bowl prize. Unfortunately, the Stranglers end up grasping for air as they suffocated in defeat from the Nightmares.

The other playoff teams were Black Mamba, Hackers, Lightning Strike, and Jokers Wild. Mamba fell to the Hackers and Lightning Strike fell to the Jokers Wild in the first round. In the second round the Hackers fall short of the Cobras and the Jokers fell short of the Mutts. The Hackers then hacked their way to victory over the Jokers to take home 3rd Place.
